# Use of the Qualis name

Qualis is free and open source software under the [Apache License 2.0](LICENSE): anyone may use it, study it, change it, fork it and redistribute it. The license covers the code. It does not cover the name: section 6 of the Apache License says that it grants no permission to use the trade names, trademarks or product names of the licensor.

This page explains how the name "Qualis" (and the project's logo, if any) may be used. Its aim is to keep "Qualis" a reliable pointer to the official project, while giving credit to it in every work that builds on it.

## Always required: credit to Qualis

Anyone who redistributes Qualis or a work derived from it must, under section 4 of the license:

- keep the copyright, patent, trademark and attribution notices of the source files;
- include a copy of the [LICENSE](LICENSE) and a readable copy of the [NOTICE](NOTICE) file, which names Qualis and links to the project;
- state, in the files they changed, that they changed them.

The NOTICE asks redistributors to keep the paragraph "This product includes Qualis, developed by The Qualis Authors: https://github.com/CERTIFIE/qualis" in their own NOTICE and, where their product shows third-party notices, among them.

## Allowed without asking

- Saying that a product, a fork or a plugin is **based on Qualis**, **uses Qualis**, **works with Qualis** or **is a plugin for Qualis**, as long as this is true.
- Using the name in articles, talks, tutorials, comparisons and documentation that refer to the project.
- Redistributing **unmodified** releases of Qualis under the name Qualis.
- Naming a plugin package after the name, such as `qualis-plugin-python` or `@acme/qualis-plugin-go`, as long as its description does not suggest that it is an official plugin when it is not.
- Keeping the name in the code of a fork: package names, import paths, commands and configuration files (`qualis`, `@qualis/*`, `qualis.config.json`) may stay as they are, so that forks remain compatible with plugins and configurations.

## Not allowed without the maintainers' permission

- Distributing a **modified** version under the name "Qualis" alone, or in a way that suggests that it is the official project. Give your fork its own product name (for example "Acme Analyzer, based on Qualis").
- Suggesting that the Qualis maintainers endorse, sponsor or certify your product, service or company.
- Using "Qualis" in the name of a company, a domain name or a social media account in a way that can be confused with the project.

## Questions

When in doubt, or to ask for a permission, open an issue on [GitHub](https://github.com/CERTIFIE/qualis/issues). This page is a statement of the project's policy, not legal advice.
